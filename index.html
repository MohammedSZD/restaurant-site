About us

A table for every kind of appetite

Food Lover is built around a simple idea: good ingredients, cooked with care and served generously. From a lazy weekend brunch to a late dinner with friends, the menu is designed to be shared.

Our kitchen keeps things honest. Dishes are cooked to order, flavours are balanced rather than loud, and there is always something for vegetarians and meat lovers alike.

  • Cooked to orderEvery plate leaves the pass fresh.
  • Made to shareBig portions and easy-going service.
  • Open lateBreakfast through to after-dinner dessert.
